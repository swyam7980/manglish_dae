Manglish translator

Clean up noisy romanised Malayalam, or write it in Malayalam script. This page runs Manglish-Tiny, a small model, entirely in your browser: nothing you type leaves your device.

Results Hugging Face Space Weights

Trained on the same 18,548 training sentences (SeLLaI Manglish, SeLLaI Mixed-Code, Dakshina) used to compare mBART-50, mT5 and ByT5, plus Dakshina's Malayalam-script pairs. On the 2,059-sentence held-out test it is as accurate as the 580M-parameter Manglish-ByT5 (2.3% character error for both); to run ByT5, download the weights and use the project's local interface.
