Manglish translator

Clean up noisy romanised Malayalam, or write it in Malayalam script. This page runs Manglish-Tiny, a small model, entirely in your browser from files on your PC: neither the model nor anything you type leaves your device.

Results

Choose the model folder. It needs encoder.int8.onnx, decoder.int8.onnx, vocab.json and config.json; in the project they are in outputs/ours_tiny_script/onnx.

Trained on the 18,548 sentences used to compare mBART-50, mT5 and ByT5, Dakshina's script pairs and the Malayalam side of about 212,000 Malayalam-English sentence pairs written in Manglish. It cleans noisy Manglish with 2.2% character error on 2,059 held-out sentences, better than the 580M-parameter Manglish-ByT5 (2.3%), and writes Malayalam script with 7.5% character error from typed and 10.1% from noisy input. English words typed into Manglish become the right Malayalam word 41% of the time.
