Limits

    Deviations from the proposal

      Two held-out sets are used: the original 325 Manglish validation sentences, and a 2,059-sentence combined test set with held-out sentences from three datasets (the 325 are part of it). Noise is synthetic and fixed per sentence. Lower CER and WER are better; higher BLEU and chrF are better. This study makes no translation-quality claim.
